# b7fbb716d189

A unique transform token `1..4` appears in any corner, independently of its
value. A connected multicolor motif appears in the interior. Crop the motif and
apply the token-selected transform: `1` identity, `2` clockwise rotation, `3`
180-degree rotation, `4` transpose.

The sampler explicitly crosses token values with all four corners, retains
canonical-size/palette support in every difficulty band, exposes six exact
observable controls, and rejects unknown controls. Its declared canonical-fit
rival uses the corner instead of reading the token; generated counterexamples
and the lesson hook prevent that shortcut from surviving every demonstration.

Kind: `per_puzzle`. Reviewer version: `3.0.0`.
