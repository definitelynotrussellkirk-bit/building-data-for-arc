### Four-profile teaching schedule

Every four consecutive samples include:

1. a canonical lattice-aligned anchor;
2. an interior off-lattice example that falsifies “fill the containing 3×3 macrocell”;
3. an edge example that demonstrates clipping; and
4. an interior overlap example that demonstrates halo union.

The profiles deliberately avoid unrelated confounds: off-lattice and edge cases keep halos disjoint, while the overlap case stays away from boundaries.
