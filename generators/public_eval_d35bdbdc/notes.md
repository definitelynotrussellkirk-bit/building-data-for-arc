## Canonical interpretation

Recover nonblack/nongray **4-connected** multicolor beads. Each has one body
color and one center (a uniform cross uses its geometric center; a clipped bead
retains its distinct center). Recover the gray wire using eight-neighbor edges,
suppressing a diagonal when a gray cardinal corner already connects it. Select
its two leaves. Endpoints must touch a bead on its center row/column, and the
bead's center must name an available donor body color. Jointly assign distinct
receivers. Copy each receiver's donor center, remove all other beads, retain gray.

The supplied final test has an endpoint adjacent to both a donor and a receiver;
only the latter has a resolvable donor pointer. Another supplied test needs the
joint distinct assignment. These are explicit grammar constraints, not a grid
lookup. All six originals, including diagonal contact between distinct beads,
uniform crosses, and a clipped bottom bead, are regression fixtures. The finite
examples do not establish a unique interpretation of every hypothetical ambiguous
wire; off-grammar endpoint ambiguity is rejected rather than silently guessed.

## Alternative and causal dependency

`transfer=follow_chain` repeatedly reads the original scene's input-defined
body-to-center dictionary. The newly read center selects the next donor. A
missing key or first repeated color terminates. The sampler includes one- through
four-link chains, shared chains, irrelevant donors, varied palette/pose/size,
squares/crosses, diagonal wire turns, and a substantial 30x30 six-bead scene.
The protected counterfactual substitutes the donor BODY for its center at each
lookup. It prematurely cycles and changes both retained centers in the witness.

## Teaching repair

The initial 64-seed audit found `largest_two` surviving both minimum demos at
seed 57, then disagreeing on a query. The first demonstration now uses small
cross receivers and larger square donors, ensuring the size shortcut is shown
wrong. Retained before/after Racket replay: 24 evaluations. The final 64-seed,
2/3/6-demo audit has 384 episodes and 8,448 named-rival checks, with no retained
failure. The declared list contains 22 policies, including both one-hop and
chain variants of geometric selection shortcuts. This is a finite self-review,
not universal rule uniqueness.

Final sampler review also removed the rigid receiver positions. Receiver and
donor roles are shuffled across six geometric slots. A bounded route constructor
visits a nonreceiver bead incidentally, so raw wire contact does not identify the
receivers. The first two scenes reverse receiver-versus-donor size ordering.
A bounded, deterministic teaching gate resamples only those first two scenes
until no declared hypothesis fits both while changing a query. This is an
explicit finite hypothesis curriculum, not an unrestricted guarantee of
identifiability. Tests check incidental contacts and at least ten of the fifteen
possible receiver-slot pairs in a retained finite sweep.
