A zero-background grid contains two to four complete copies of one connected 4–9-cell polyomino and exactly one connected defective strict subset missing one or two cells. The output is the complete normalized polyomino painted with the defective copy's color.

Version 4.0.0 keeps exact canonical-profile anchors, but alternates them with color-transfer examples in which the defect has a distinct color. Every three-example lesson—and every one-query rotation of a four-pair bundle—therefore refutes the canonical-fitting shortcut “crop a largest component.” Easy, medium, and hard contrast examples use progressively larger templates, fewer redundant full copies, and more missing cells. Four exact observable overrides are supported: `profile`, `template_size`, `n_canon`, and `missing`.
