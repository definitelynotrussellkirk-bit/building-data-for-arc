Square 0-background grids with 3-5 distinct colored layers. Each color's largest 4-connected component has a unique bbox span; spans descend by two. Layers are hollow square/rectangular frame remnants, except a 1x1 or 2x2 innermost block. Layouts may be scattered, overlapping, edge-clipped, or include exactly one detached same-color singleton that forces largest-component selection. The rule restacks the spans as centered concentric square outlines, largest outside.
