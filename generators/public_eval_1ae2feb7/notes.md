# public_eval_1ae2feb7 — current authoring design

General rule: the unique long nonzero vertical separator divides a short run-code
from a blank output field. Each nonzero run supplies its color and its length as
a pulse period. Pulses begin adjacent to the separator; nearer source runs win
collisions. Rows may face left or right. Zero gaps do not contribute a period.

Native setting: pulse_phase=leading (parent) or trailing (last position of each
period). Derive ordered run records first, then composite their periodic masks.

four-query bank; presentation shuffles the full bank independently before slicing.

finite shortcut elimination at two demos; Racket parity including 30x30; protected
wrong-period witness; one-query privacy and independently verified content IDs.
