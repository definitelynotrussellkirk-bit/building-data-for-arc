Every maximal vertical green (`3`) run of **exact length 2** is recolored
orange (`7`). Runs of length 1 or 3+ remain green.

The production curriculum uses a six-slot alternating schedule. Every four-pair
lesson contains two scenes with a length-4 negative and two without one, while
covering length-1, length-3, and length-5 controls. This defeats both the
canonical-fitting shortcut “recolor every even-length run” and the distribution
shortcut “apply the rule only when length 4 is present.” Exact public axes are
grid height, grid width, and number of target runs.
