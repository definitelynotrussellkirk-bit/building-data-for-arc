## Implemented curriculum

Version 3.0.0 uses a four-residue schedule over those exact canonical
profiles. Palette sizes 6–8 imply one to three distinct distractor colors;
each distractor appears once. Matching pixels cover at least three of the four
routes and include a repeated route. A deterministic bipartite placement step
keeps source cells distinct while guaranteeing collision-free destination
coordinates even at exact override boundaries.

The retained semantic contract names six witnesses and five rivals:

- witnesses: all four route directions, distractor removal, and repeated-route
  multiplicity;
- rivals: clear all interior cells, keep distractors, move only the first pixel
  per route, swap top/bottom, and swap left/right.

Every sliding three-demonstration lesson generated by the focused tests rejects
all named rivals on its query.

## Exact overrides

constructible:

- `grid_h`: 8..18

# Decisions: 8ce1331089a3

## D1 — Treat non-border colors as required teaching evidence

**Original rationale:** Unknown; the imported generator stated that all
interior colors matched a border, even though every canonical input contradicts
that statement.

**Choice:** Generate `palette_size - 5` distinct distractor colors (one to
three), each exactly once, and require them in `self_check`.

**Alternatives considered:** Keep distractors optional; generate repeated
non-border blobs; remove them only in hard mode.

**Probe:** Canonical delta analysis showed distractor counts 2, 1, 1, and 3.
The old generator had zero in every draw. The new rule mirror removes every
nonmatching color, and `keep_distractors` is a named rival.

**Failure modes / limits:** Requiring each distractor once is faithful to the
four official pairs but may be narrower than the latent task family. Exact
palette sizes remain canonical (6–8).

## D2 — Cycle the four exact canonical profiles by default

**Original rationale:** The old random 8–18 distribution had no linked profile
support and generated a fixed five-color palette.

**Choice:** `sample_index % 4` selects one official
`(height,width,palette,n_pixels)` profile in every difficulty band.

**Alternatives considered:** Broad random marginals; weighted sampling around
the canonical envelope; one profile per difficulty.

**Probe:** Final profile gate accepted all eight canonical copies and found
20/20 exact-profile draws in each band. The local probe saw each profile 10/40
per band.

**Failure modes / limits:** Default morphology diversity is four profiles.
Broader values are explicit overrides rather than default training data.

## D3 — Separate difficulty from profile support

**Original rationale:** The initial rewrite accidentally made difficulty inert;
the first strict after battery caught byte-identical easy/medium/hard draws.

**Choice:** Difficulty rotates the route-balance and placement curriculum while
shape, palette, and count continue to cycle canonically.

**Alternatives considered:** Difficulty-specific dimensions; increasing pixel
counts; no difficulty argument.

**Probe:** A test was written first and observed failing; after the change, the
same seed/index produces three distinct grids whose observed canonical profile
is identical. `defect_lint` then passed.

**Failure modes / limits:** Difficulty is pedagogical, not an ordinal guarantee
that every hard sample is objectively harder for every solver.

## D4 — Reduce AXES to four exact observable controls

**Original rationale:** The old surface exposed density, palette/texture aliases,
position biases, a singleton palette size, and degenerate textures. Several
were ignored, clamped, overlapped, or incompatible with difficulty bands.

**Choice:** Keep only `grid_h`, `grid_w`, `n_pixels`, and `palette_size`, with
ranges 8–18, 8–18, 6–16, and 6–8. Reject unknown names and non-integers.

**Alternatives considered:** Repair every legacy axis; retain position style as
an enum; declare large incompatibility tables.

**Probe:** 501 override cases × three bands × 30 seeds produced 45,090 accepted
and exactly observed grids, with unknown-key rejection and no declared
incompatibilities.

**Failure modes / limits:** Placement mode is no longer a public override. This
is intentional: it is curriculum-internal and not necessary for reproducing
canonical morphology.

## D5 — Use constructive matching instead of retry placement

**Original rationale:** Rejection sampling can silently underfill and becomes
fragile at small grids or high exact counts.

**Choice:** Assign unique destination coordinates per route, then solve source
cell assignment using deterministic bipartite matching.

**Alternatives considered:** Retry loops with caps; prepartitioning the interior
by route; rejecting dense combinations.

**Probe:** All boundary combinations in the strict override battery are
constructible, including 8×8 grids with 16 interior pixels and palette size 8.

**Failure modes / limits:** The recursive augmenting-path matcher is more code
than random retries, though inputs are tiny (at most 16 tokens).

## D6 — Require at least three routes and a repeated route

**Original rationale:** A lesson containing too few directions or one pixel per
route leaves direction swaps or first-only movement insufficiently tested.

**Choice:** Route allocation starts with all four when possible and then adds
repeated routes under collision-free capacity constraints. `self_check`
requires at least three route types.

**Alternatives considered:** Require all four unconditionally; let the semantic
census establish coverage only across a lesson rather than per sample.

**Probe:** All six semantic witnesses and all five rival disagreements fired on
every one of 120 local-probe draws; the strict semantic gate passed 600 draws.

**Failure modes / limits:** Per-sample witness density may make the intended
rule easier than sparse canonical evidence. The layouts and colors still vary.

## D11 — Add an exact-default native parametric family with a real sequential dependency

**Choice:** Keep `path_rule=wall_endpoint` byte-for-byte identical to the
canonical Racket program and add `path_rule=dominant_trace`.  For the alternate
setting, Step A counts the four border-color routes and chooses the unique
maximum; its intermediate is the selected route plus all four counts.  Step B
uses that result to decide which matching source pixels expand into complete
source-to-wall traces.  Non-selected routes retain canonical wall endpoints and
non-route distractors disappear.

**Why this is non-collapsing:** The expanded route changes with each input.  A
fixed route cannot implement Step B before the counts are known.  The retained
wrong-intermediate witness forces `top` on a case whose count-derived route is
`bottom`; protected Racket produces a different final grid.

**Contract:** The task-local adapter supports 2–6 demonstrations and 1–4
queries, uses independent deterministic streams so prefixes are stable, gives
the two settings identical inputs, and reserves a substantive 30×30 fourth
query.  All generated inputs have a unique dominant route and one to three
non-border distractor colors.

**Evidence:** `workspace/parametric/evidence/manifest.json` binds the adapter,
program resources, tests, and compact audit evidence.  Native qualification at
seeds 0 and 17 with three demonstrations and four queries is in
`checks/8ce1331089a3/qualification/`; the causal witness is in
`checks/8ce1331089a3/causal/witness.json`.  Qualified program hashes are
`253732acf6736feb1c912003b457ef831e22d176809bbe87cd11e537ef6a3545`
(canonical) and
`d9dd0e671ec382cef6dc8485af15b1fab7f41b358bf6636b708f8d7c6dec9f8c`
(dominant trace).

**Limits:** Qualification and the seven-rival self-audit are finite.  They do
not prove universal identifiability, owner acceptance, or measured training
gain.  The production generator remains unchanged at SHA-256
