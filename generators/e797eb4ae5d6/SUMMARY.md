One green(3) seed anchors a fixed stencil made of a 49-cell spiral path and a disconnected 11-cell lower bar. With no color-2 blockers, every in-bounds zero stencil cell is painted green. If any color-2 blocker exists anywhere, only the blocker-free stencil component 4-connected to the seed is painted: an on-path blocker cuts the spiral, while even an off-stencil blocker suppresses the disconnected bar. Scheduled lessons cover the no-blocker mask, the global off-mask switch, early/late cuts, varied blocker counts, shifted anchors, clipping, and varied grid sizes.
