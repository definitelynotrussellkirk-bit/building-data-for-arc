# Three disjoint recoloring rules

The private `recolor_scope` is selected once per episode: `all_sources`
(exact canonical default), `most_frequent_source` (the retained selective rule),
or `mapped_region_relay` (the exact rule from review-adf929d968f3fb1a).
All three programs retain their reviewed bytes. No Cartesian combinations are
introduced. Original native.py and its sampler remain intact.

All settings share relay scenes with a two-row legend, blank header separator,
a color-9 dividing column, a left controller and three color-6 payload objects.
Relay applies the full legend, counts resulting color-2 regions on the left,
and recolors the corresponding payload to 8. Most-frequent-source applies only
the legend pair whose source has the unique greatest body count.

The common input grammar intentionally extends the older sampler: target-2
cells already occur in the body, and passthrough colors 6, 8 and 9 coexist.
The old Racket frequency rule is total on this grammar. The union mirror uses
this grammar explicitly instead of weakening the retained old validator.
Replacing inert filler-8 cells with source 3 or 5 varies the frequency winner
while preserving controller geometry, payload geometry and visible occupancy.
The first two demos retain matched nuisance statistics and different region
counts; their source-frequency winners differ as well.

Supports 2–6 prefix-stable demos and 1–4 prefix-stable queries, default four.
The fourth input is 30×30 and its output is 27×30 after removing the header.

old selector rivals and twelve incoming relay rivals; 104 protected-Racket
labels cover intended programs and selected executable rivals at seeds 0/17.
