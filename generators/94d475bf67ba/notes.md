## D3. Domain: exact, monochrome, strictly nested outlines

**Choice.** Generate exactly two or three distinct nonzero colors, each forming one connected exact rectangular perimeter. Child rectangles are strictly inside parents with at least one full zero-cell ring.

## D6. Four-slot deterministic curriculum

**Choice.** `sample_index mod 4` selects `(11,11,2)`, `(12,12,3)`, `(10,13,2)`, or `(12,14,3)`. `EXAMPLES_NEEDED = 4`.
