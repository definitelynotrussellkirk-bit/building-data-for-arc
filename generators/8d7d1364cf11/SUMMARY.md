On a background-4 grid, two or three separated multicolor components are source
templates and isolated non-background cells are dots. Each source contains one
globally unique accent color. A dot matching an accent stamps the complete
source template on a fresh background-4 output with that accent aligned to the
dot. Dots whose colors occur in no source are decoys and disappear; source
templates and original dots are not preserved.

The v3.0.0 sampler uses 16–24 cell canvases, six recoverable shape-family
schedules, independent D4 orientations, one or two copies per source, one to
three decoys, and both shared- and distinct-body palettes. Seven exact controls
are recoverable from pixels. A canonical-core profile preserves 18×18 support,
while broad profiles invert accidental canonical color correlations—most
notably by making color 5 productive.

Training lessons contain three demonstrations and one query and are checked
against five executable semantic competitors. The selected canonical Racket
rule remains unchanged at `0a9706fafc20`; selected base copies are synchronized
with it.
