## D3 — Generate only structurally lawful pairs

**Choice.** Every ordinary pair has two endpoints of one nonzero color, one row
or column, even span >=2, an empty midpoint, and a midpoint distinct from every
other pair. Candidate placement is deterministic backtracking over critical
cells.

**Reason.** The baseline `self_check` verified only “two cells per color,” so
odd spans, diagonals, occupied/shared midpoints, and incomplete transformations

prevents order-dependent collisions.

**Rejected alternatives.** Random rejection was simpler but could silently
underfill hard joint controls. Allowing malformed “degenerate textures” in the
ordinary domain would teach no-op or ambiguous examples.
