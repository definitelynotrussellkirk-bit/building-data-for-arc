## What changed

The old generator exposed stale texture/palette ideas, mixed ordinary and negative cases, trusted color counts instead of geometry, and used a capped rejection loop. It also lacked a rule pin. The replacement advertises only `grid_h`, `grid_w`, and `pairs`; validates calls strictly; and chooses exact nonoverlapping endpoint/midpoint triples by backtracking. Every generated color occurs exactly twice and every requested pair is realized.

The first pair is a lawful span-2 witness, with orientation alternating by sample index. A second pair, when present, has canonical span 4 in the opposite orientation. This refutes the strongest canonical shortcut without losing examples of the demonstrated case. The exact disagreement input is retained at `repo/generators/1e26763a6f70/counterexamples/span_four_only.json`.

A deterministic anchor every third sample supplies an exact observed canonical shape/pair-count profile. The earlier every-fifth design was rejected after the profile-floor gate produced only 4/20 exact profiles in one band; that failed evidence remains at `checks/profile_floor/`, and the corrected pass is at `checks/profile-floor-v2/`.

## Self-review for new restrictions or shortcuts

The repair deliberately narrows the truthful generator API, not the selected rule: it omits malformed/negative examples from ordinary sampling and bounds lessons to five pairs and the advertised canvases. The span-2 witness prevents the canonical span-4-only rival, while the optional span-4 opposite-orientation pair prevents a lesson from implying that only the new witness span or one orientation is valid. The semantic census is evidence only against the named rival; unknown shortcuts remain possible.
