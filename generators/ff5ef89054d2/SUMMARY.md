A sparse 9..11 square-or-near-square grid contains two to four isolated colored singleton markers. Each singleton gains its in-bounds diagonal neighbors in the same color, forming a full or boundary-clipped X. Some profiles also contain one separated 2..3-cell connected decoy, which is not isolated and therefore remains unchanged.

Version `3.1.0` preserves two strict canonical-like profiles and places decoys in profiles 2..5, so every three-demonstration lesson and every single-query rotation retains negative evidence against expanding connected nonzero cells.
