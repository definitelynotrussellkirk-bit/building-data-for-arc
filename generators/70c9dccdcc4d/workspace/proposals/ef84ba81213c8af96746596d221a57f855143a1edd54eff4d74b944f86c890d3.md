A separate non-symmetric near motif refutes copying `(u,v)` without swapping. The exact Racket-checked witness is `checks/shortcut/witness.json`; the Racket execution record is `checks/shortcut/racket.json`.

Constructive cached 10x14 templates cover all 64 source/target orientation combinations and up to four endpoint-disjoint distant witnesses. Larger requested grids shift a valid template rather than relying on rejection sampling. Defaults are weighted toward the canonical 12x18/eight-color profile while preserving the full exact domains. Profile support improved from 2/60 pooled hits with zero easy/hard support to 19/60, with at least one exact hit in every band.
