Constructive same-color pair-span curriculum. Inputs contain 1–4 distinct
non-zero colors, each appearing exactly twice, on 7×7 through 13×13 grids.
Pairs are either all axis-aligned or all perfect 45-degree diagonals; the output
is the union of their inclusive spans, recolored to 8 on a blank canvas.

Kind: `per_puzzle`. Current version: `2.0.0`. Canonical rule hash:
`4a7acff260a8`. The five exact controls are grid height, grid width, pair count,
low/high palette, and orientation family. A declared fixed-color-role shortcut
fits the canonical examples and is explicitly refuted by generated lessons.
Approval remains derived by the catalog from current code and retained evidence.
