# f83a50ff3464 — Orthogonal Center Fill

Fill an interior zero cell exactly when its up, down, left, and right neighbors
are one identical nonzero color. All other cells remain unchanged.

The version 3.0.0 generator uses twelve exact observable scene profiles, four
per difficulty band. Each four-example curriculum cycle contains a
canonical-like clean board plus targeted counterexamples to four rules that fit
every canonical pair: a 3-of-4 majority, equal opposite pairs, three visible
same-color arms, and only one completed center per color. Profiles stay inside
the canonical 7–9 row/column envelope and vary true-center count, repeated
colors, one explicit near miss, and at most two isolated distractors.

Kind: `per_puzzle`. Selected rule hash: `14c360998abe`.
