Place 1–8 distinct colors as pairs of opposite corners of pairwise-disjoint 3×3 boxes on a black 5–30 by 5–30 canvas. Each pair may use the main or anti diagonal. The canonical transformation fills both diagonals of every box, turning each two-corner clue into a five-cell X of the same color.

Public generator axes are `grid_h`, `grid_w`, exact `n_pairs`, and `texture=main_only|anti_only|mixed`. Explicit impossible combinations are rejected rather than silently changed. The separate proposed parametric family adds an orientation-dependent X/plus completion while preserving the exact canonical default.
