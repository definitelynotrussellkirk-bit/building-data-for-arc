A zero-background grid contains at least two nonzero colours whose two cells are diagonally opposite corners of separate axis-aligned rectangles. Fill every cell of each implied rectangle with that pair's colour. Some lessons also contain a different colour appearing twice on one row or one column; that collinear pair stays unchanged because it is not diagonal.
