Five or six distinctly colored four-connected objects are placed on a compact
7–8 by 7–10 canvas. Two objects are neighbors when their minimum Manhattan
cell distance is at most two. Every object is recolored by its number of
neighboring objects: degree 0→2, 1→3, 2→4, 3→5, 4→6, and 5+→7.

The ordinary five-example curriculum cycles through pair, chain, 3-spoke hub,
4-spoke hub, and dense contact graphs. Together they cover degrees 0 through 5.
Singleton and mixed-shape variants distinguish counting neighboring objects
from counting nearby cells. The lawful all-isolated profile remains available
as an exact override and canonical edge case, but is not scheduled as a normal
teaching slot.
