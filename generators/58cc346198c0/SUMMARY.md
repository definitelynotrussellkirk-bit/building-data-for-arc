For each separated nonzero connected object, erase all of its cells and paint exactly the four corners of that object's bounding box in the object's original color. Object identity is determined by 4-connectivity rather than color identity: ordinary lessons deliberately include two separated objects with the same color. The curriculum alternates mixed and single shape families and grows irregular connected polyominoes procedurally instead of drawing from a small fixed template bank.
