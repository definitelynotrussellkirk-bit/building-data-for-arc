`57674fc89a33`. A nonzero mirrored value wins: different colors at mirrored
positions swap. All five canonical pairs match this program, but they contain

destinations, maximum-color merging, and sequential in-place copying—therefore
fit every canonical pair. The 2×1 input `[[1],[2]]` separates all three from the
rule's `[[2],[1]]` result.

program and the existing baseline generator, not a claim that the canonical
