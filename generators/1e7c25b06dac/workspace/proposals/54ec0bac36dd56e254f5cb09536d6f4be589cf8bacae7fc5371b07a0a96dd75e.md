## What changed and why

The old sampler always used `(3,1)`, `(4,2)`, and `(n-1,1)` and three distinct
colors. A lower-to-upper-only or fixed-coordinate program could therefore solve
all ordinary generated examples without learning the intended bidirectional
transpose-fill behavior. The validator rejected four of the five canonical
inputs because they had four or five nonzero cells. The repair varies positions
and counts, always includes an informative source in each triangle, and supports
diagonal anchors and repeated colors. Invalid cells, unsupported controls,
conflicting square aliases, and coercible numeric/string values fail explicitly.

Large grids contain newly sampled source geometry across the requested square,
not a padded small pattern. Validated production controls include `grid_size=30`
and equal `grid_h`/`grid_w` aliases, through the normal four-query generation
route. `palette_size=1..9` describes available colors. Identity-only diagnostic
textures stay rejected as ordinary lessons. Occupied conflicting mirror partners
were checked against the reference separately; the ordinary sampler does not
claim to emit them. Current decisions and exact runnable commands are in the
selected task's `notes.md`.

The reusable `workspace/verify_transpose.py` executes canonical gold pairs,
requested-size lessons, and the occupied-partner boundary through Racket. It
records exact source/rule hashes, accepted indices, labels, and finite-rival
certificates. It makes later source changes independently reviewable.

placement masks, and swatches aligned to the top templates. These roles cannot
be expressed by E38's simple cell reflection; the new opt-in prototype remains
separate. Its parameter transposes coarse tile positions while preserving each
template's pixel orientation. Both settings share the same seven input grids,
with three demonstrations and four distinct queries per setting.

All retained labels come from materialized Racket programs. Identity reproduces
all three supplied public demonstrations exactly. The two public query inputs
were executed, but their official answers are absent. Demonstrations alone
eliminate the alternative settings and five semantic shortcuts for each retained
query; the certificate does not use query answers to choose a rule. Public
filenames hash exact visible bytes, and private settings, seeds, programs, and
answers do not affect those names. Direct API output grids now reject booleans,
floats, malformed dimensions, and invalid colors before certification/export.

Current prototype evidence is **only** `checks/parametric/final-current/`;
earlier retained folders have superseded source or documentation bindings.
The final README, source, tests, and exact Racket programs match that report.
