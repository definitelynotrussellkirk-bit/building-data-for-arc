# 1e7c25b06dac

Main-diagonal mirror add: fill a blank cell from its transposed partner and
preserve every occupied cell. The canonical Racket rule is unchanged.

Version 2.1.0 samples square grids with varied source positions in both triangles,
variable counts, optional diagonal anchors, and repeated colors. Exact square
size overrides support 3..30; default sizes are 6..30. Identity-only diagnostic
textures remain outside the ordinary lesson domain. Occupied reciprocal pairs
are preserved by the reference but are not emitted by the sampler.

Kind: `per_puzzle`. Source approval remains an owner decision.
A separate proposed template-expansion prototype is under `workspace/parametric/`;
it does not replace this canonical family or admit data to training.
