A 0-background grid contains two or three blank centers whose four diagonal neighbors share one nonzero color. The output fills every such center with that color and preserves all other cells. Each generated lesson also contains one controlled non-answer: only three matching diagonals, four nonzero but mismatched diagonals, or a nonzero center surrounded by four matching diagonals.

Version 3.1.0 moves motifs off the former fixed `1 mod 3` lattice, schedules two shared-color examples per three-example lesson without tying reuse to any distractor type, and gives one positive center a harmless opposite orthogonal context pair. These witnesses reject fixed-position, one-center-per-color, distractor-conditioned, and blank-orthogonal shortcuts while retaining the canonical local rule.
