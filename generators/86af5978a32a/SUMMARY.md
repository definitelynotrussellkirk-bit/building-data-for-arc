Compose two equal square panels of side 3–5 either horizontally or vertically. The panel with 2–4 distinct nonzero colors is the layout; the panel with exactly one nonzero color is a binary shape mask. For every nonzero layout cell, stamp the mask into the corresponding output block and recolor it with that layout color, yielding a `k² × k²` output.
