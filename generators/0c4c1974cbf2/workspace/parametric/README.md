# Native folded-pair family

`pair_mode=overlap` is the exact canonical rule: compare reflected cells across
the centered color-8 bar and paint red on the right only when both are occupied.
`pair_mode=exclusive` keeps the same first stage but paints exactly-one-occupied
pairs. Inputs are matched across settings and counts, the first two demonstrations
distinguish the modes, and four private queries include a dense 30x29 boundary
stress scene. The canonical production generator and authority are unchanged.
