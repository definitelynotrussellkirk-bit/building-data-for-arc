# da16c00eed25 — Complete the Rectangle Corner

A 4..14 by 4..14 zero-background grid contains exactly three cells of one
nonzero color. They occupy three of the four products of two distinct rows and
two distinct columns. The rule paints the unique missing row-column product
with the same color and leaves every other cell unchanged.

The sampler deliberately covers cases absent from the canonical demonstrations:

- row or column gaps of exactly one;
- all four possible missing corners;
- rectangles touching every grid boundary;
- canonical-sized/palette-compatible examples in easy, medium, and hard bands.

Ordinary four-example lessons use a four-slot cyclic schedule. One slot is a
joint counterexample to the three declared canonical-fitting shortcuts
(`min_gap_two`, `bottom_missing_only`, and `interior_only`); the other slots
retain broad random geometry. Every four consecutive demonstrations therefore
contains a labeled rebuttal while preserving varied positions, dimensions,
colors, gaps, and missing corners.

Kind: `per_puzzle`. Current version: `3.0.0`. Canonical rule hash:
