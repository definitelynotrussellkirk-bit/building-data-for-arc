Zero-background grid with distinct-color top-row markers and 1..10 monochrome
four-connected bodies below an empty row 1. Recolor each body to the marker
nearest its horizontal bounding-box center; exact ties choose the smaller
marker column. Markers and background persist. Body colors are disjoint from
marker colors, while diagonal same-color contacts are allowed.

Kind: `per_puzzle`. Current proposed version: `2.2.0`.
Approval is derived by the catalog from current code and retained evidence.
