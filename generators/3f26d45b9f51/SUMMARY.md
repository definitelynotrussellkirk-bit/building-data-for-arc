A square `(3*K-1) × (3*K-1)` lattice of `K × K` uniform 2×2 cells is separated by one-pixel divider rows and columns. The modal nonzero lattice color is a divider role, not a fixed color. For every marker color independently, fill the complete inclusive coarse-cell interval between the outermost same-color markers in each shared coarse row or column. A color can connect on both axes; singleton and off-line markers remain unchanged.

Kind: `per_puzzle`. Current version: `4.0.0`.

Version 4 adds an ordinary three-collinear-marker witness that distinguishes full min-to-max filling from a nearest-adjacent-pair shortcut, rejects coercible non-integer exact-axis overrides, and declares the 12-slot deterministic schedule period.
