# fb1e79984e59

Generate a 5..18 by 5..18 grid whose Chebyshev-depth rings are each uniform.  Four scheduled ring grammars are used: all distinct colors, innermost repeats outermost, innermost repeats its neighbor, and a two-color alternation.  A six-step schedule covers every grammar; every three consecutive demonstrations contain both an all-distinct and an outer/inner repeated-ring witness.  Zero is alternately included and excluded.  The oracle lists diagonal ring colors outer-to-inner, removes duplicate colors preserving first occurrence, and repaints every color with the preceding unique color cyclically.
