Sparse zero-background grids containing two or three nonzero colors. For each
color, every pair of markers sharing a row or column induces a filled span
between the extreme markers on that axis. Existing nonzero cells are preserved;
when spans overlap on a background cell, the numerically smallest eligible color
wins, matching the pinned canonical Racket rule.

The production curriculum alternates a comprehensive teaching case with a varied
case. Teaching cases include multi-marker and multi-span structure, a lower-color
overlap, and an unaligned distractor, jointly refuting the declared
`exactly_two_only`, `first_pair_only`, `highest_color_wins`, and
`bounding_box_connect` shortcuts. Varied cases include pairs, collinear triples,
rectangles, crossings, and distractors. Grid sizes are selected from 7×9, 8×8,
10×12, 14×14, and 18×18; the canonical-scale 7×9/8×8 profiles recur in every
difficulty band.

Kind: `per_puzzle`. Reviewed version: `3.0.0`. Canonical `RULE_HASH`:
`bde71b91a345`. Approval remains a catalog/maintainer decision; passing review
checks does not itself admit the generator to training.
