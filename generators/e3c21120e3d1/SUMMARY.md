Transpose the entire sparse rectangular grid across its main diagonal, so
`output[c][r] = input[r][c]` and an `H×W` input becomes `W×H`.

The version 2.0.0 curriculum uses four canonical-backed sparse-component
profiles and three exact axes (`grid_h`, `grid_w`, `n_colors`).  Every generated
input distinguishes transpose from the other seven dihedral transforms, and
three-demo lessons expose all scheduled morphology branches.
