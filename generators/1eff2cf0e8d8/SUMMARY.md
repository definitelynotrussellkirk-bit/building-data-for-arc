# 1eff2cf0e8d8 — Template Copies

A zero-background grid contains one sparse non-`{0,9}` template near the upper-left and one to three color-9 marker anchors. Crop the template to its bounding box, clear the canvas, and stamp every nonzero template cell at the original bounding-box position and at each marker, treating the marker as the copy's top-left corner. A marker is not preserved when the template's top-left cell is blank.

The version 3 curriculum uses a four-example cyclic schedule: a canonical-profile anchor, a top-left-hole marker-erasure example, an offset-source example, and a multicolor/multiple-copy example. The latter three refute canonical-fitting or otherwise plausible shortcuts.
