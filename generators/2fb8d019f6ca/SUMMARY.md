# Stamp the Extracted Template

Extract the four-connected colored component rooted at (0,0), excluding 0 and 8.
Stamp its colored cells onto a blank canvas at every color-8 anchor. The repaired
sampler combines canonical-size cases with longer templates, disconnected
diagonal distractors, exact palette/anchor counts and collision-free placement.
See notes.md for supported controls, teaching guarantees and explicit limits.
