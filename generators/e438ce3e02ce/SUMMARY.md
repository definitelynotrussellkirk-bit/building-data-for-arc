A 9..10 by 9..12 zero-background grid contains 3..5 singleton seed cells in
pairwise-distinct colors excluding 0 and 8. For every zero cell, compute
Manhattan distance to all seeds. A unique nearest seed supplies its color; a tie
between two or more nearest seeds becomes cyan (8). Seed cells remain fixed.

Normal examples use an eight-slot teaching schedule. Every cyclic four-example
window includes 3-, 4-, and 5-seed cases, Manhattan-vs-Euclidean discriminators
at every seed count, a metric-equivalent control, a genuine three-way tie, and
examples where the fourth and fifth seeds change blank regions.

Kind: `per_puzzle`. Imported version: `4.0.0`.
