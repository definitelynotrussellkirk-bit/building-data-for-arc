A rectangular grid has a closed one-cell gray (5) frame, zero interior background, and three or four distinct non-{0,5,8} seed colors. Fill each interior zero with the color of its uniquely nearest seed by Manhattan distance; if two or more seeds tie for the minimum distance, paint cyan (8). Preserve the seed cells and the gray frame.

The eight-slot curriculum contains, in every four-sample window, three- and four-seed controls plus Manhattan-versus-Euclidean discriminators at both seed counts. Metric-equivalent controls now include both collinear and non-collinear seed layouts, preventing collinearity from becoming a proxy for which metric is safe.
