This favors a demonstrated reusable teaching contract and a specific current repair over unbounded volume expansion. The time split is an estimate and hypothesis, not a measured quality optimum. The global 10% focus, official ticket weights and dataset mix weights stay unchanged.

A four-query episode with three demonstrations needs seven distinct Racket-labeled grids for four supervised targets. Four separate one-query episodes with three demonstrations each need sixteen labeled grids for the same four targets: a 56.25% reduction in grid-label work and demonstration storage before other costs. It does not establish equivalent training-token savings; downstream context repetition, deduplication, runtime reuse and model benefit were not measured.

A bounded follow-up pilot should attempt four accepted episodes for each approved sampled family, at most eight attempted episodes per family. At three demonstrations and four queries this is 56 labels for eight accepted episodes, with a hard ceiling of 112 labels across rejected attempts. Require source/rule identity, valid known controls, four queries distinct from all demonstrations, authoritative labels and demonstration-only rejection of named relevant rivals. Stop that family's pilot immediately on a label disagreement or source drift; stop further volume expansion if acceptance, distinctness or teaching checks fail within the attempt cap.
