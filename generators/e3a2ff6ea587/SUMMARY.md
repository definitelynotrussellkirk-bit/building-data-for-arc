# e3a2ff6ea587

A zero-background grid contains one to three true rectangle-corner colors and one to three deliberately nonqualifying colors. In first-appearance order, a color with exactly four cells at exactly the corners of its bounding box fills that box into a fresh output; all other colors disappear.

The ordinary curriculum includes three close negative classes—three corners, four misplaced cells, and four corners plus an extra cell—so neither “fill every nonzero bounding box” nor “fill every four-cell bounding box” solves it. Controls are strict and observable.

Kind: `per_puzzle`. Imported version: `4.0.0`.
