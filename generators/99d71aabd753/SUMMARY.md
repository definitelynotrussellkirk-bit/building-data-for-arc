Place 3-4 ordinary-draw colors as separated solid rectangles on a zero background. Each color has a distinct number of 4-connected objects. Rectangle areas vary independently of those counts, and the colored-cell ranking conflicts with the object-count ranking. The Racket rule counts objects per color, sorts counts descending, and emits a right-aligned staircase with one row per color.
