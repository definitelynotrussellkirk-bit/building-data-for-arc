## Domain and controls

The sampler uses background 0, one unambiguous odd-width frame, and conflict-free
interior reflection orbits. Frame and content colors are geometric roles: the
frame may use any color 1–9, content excludes that frame color, and content color
1 is supported. Three to six distinct off-axis orbits prevent accidental no-op
draws; a palette of one to three colors teaches repeated-color completion.
Zero to two axis cells and zero to three exterior pixels are unchanged by the rule.

All ten advertised axes are observable from pixels. Exact overrides retain their
full declared ranges in every difficulty band; invalid types, unknown keys and
infeasible joint dimensions are rejected explicitly. Unpinned defaults adapt to
available space, including a canvas completely occupied by the frame. A request
for positive exterior noise in that full-canvas case is incompatible.

The validator checks grid shape, integer colors, the actual outline, an untied
largest frame, odd width and nonconflicting interior partners. Metadata cannot
bypass it. It accepts same-color completed pairs alongside off-axis content.
It deliberately does not claim the entire domain on which the Racket program can
execute: even-width frames, conflicting partners and interior frame-color marks
are outside this sampler's documented domain.
