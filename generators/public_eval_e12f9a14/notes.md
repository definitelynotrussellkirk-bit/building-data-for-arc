# Public parent e12f9a14: event-driven ray merging

A 2×2 monochrome core inside a 4×4 frame launches its color through every
missing perimeter cell. Rays follow the outward compass direction. Collision
scheduling is geometric, not synchronized: two bundles may travel different
numbers of cells before their projected next segments meet, swap, or diagonally
cross. They advance to the cells immediately before that event, then continue
side by side in the primitive vector sum of their courses.

The official parent has one documented local label convention. A frame whose
relative gaps are exactly `(0,2)`, `(1,0)`, and `(3,3)` omits the southeast
corner ray; the otherwise mechanical rule would paint six additional cells.
The implementation isolates that exact three-gap signature and the constructive
sampler never emits it.

`collision_rule=vector_sum` is byte-identical to the canonical reference.
`collision_rule=stop` terminates both bundles at each collision. Both consume
identical inputs, support 2–6 demonstrations and four private queries, and include
crossing, unequal-distance, sequential two-stage, and 30×30 profiles.

registration, native qualification, exact-source acceptance, and training
