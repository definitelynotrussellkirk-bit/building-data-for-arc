One to four cross-bodied creatures move opposite their blue tails until their
whole bounding boxes meet the wall. Opposite arm colors swap; every observed
tail translates intact.

Kind: per_puzzle. Current version: 2.0.0.

The sampler covers canonical small grids and multiple creatures in every band,
varies exact palettes and independently observed tails, and rejects invalid or
incompatible overrides. Structural parsing and a three-phase lesson schedule
support five explicit rival checks, including both canonical-fitting
tail-template shortcuts.

Canonical Racket supplies labels. Approval is derived from current source and
retained evidence; source changes do not inherit an earlier approval.
