## What changed

### Ordered-path curriculum

The selected authority finds the endpoint color, starts at the first row-major
endpoint, and performs BFS with neighbor order `up, down, left, right`. The
repair does not sanitize away ties. Instead, even sample indices deliberately
contain multiple shortest paths where a wrong order selects a different path;
odd indices contain a unique shortest path. Every accepted draw also has at
least one turn and at least two off-path zeros, so wall use and output clearing
are visible.

The canonical-fitting rival uses `down, right, left, up`. It reproduces all four
public pairs but disagrees on every generated tied profile in the independent
480-draw probe. Additional executable rivals cover painting all shortest cells,
ignoring walls with a row-first route, painting all open cells, and preserving
the input.

### Distribution and controls

Default shapes follow a deterministic period eight:
`5x5, 5x6, 4x7, 6x8, 5x6, 5x5, 4x8, 6x7`. The schedule provides canonical
anchors without collapsing to one size. The only public overrides are exact
observable `h`, `w`, and endpoint `color`; all legal values work in all three
difficulty bands. Unknown keys, booleans, floats, strings, and out-of-range
values fail closed.

### Reusable tooling

`repo/generators/6a289c5b0897/workspace/reference_probe.py` is an independent,
deterministic audit tool. It reimplements the selected oracle rather than using
`rule_mirror`, reads the generator's actual rival declarations dynamically,
checks every canonical pair, samples/replays the generator, and exhausts every
period start and query rotation. Its report is source-hash-bound and contains no
timestamp, so identical source and arguments produce identical bytes.

## Self-review and limitations

The repair could have introduced a parity or dimension shortcut. The exhaustive
probe therefore checked every period start and query rotation, and the 480-draw
sample covered six shapes and all seven endpoint colors; no declared rival
survived. A constructed non-production lesson with three independently sampled
unique demos and a tied query is still ambiguous (`checks/qwen9-bad-lesson.json`),
which is why exporters must preserve/test assembled lesson policy rather than
shuffle pairs blindly.

Finite executable rivals are not a proof that every human- or model-plausible
rule is eliminated. The supported domain intentionally excludes disconnected
open islands, fewer than three gray walls, dimensions outside 4..6 by 5..8, and
endpoint colors 0/5/9. Those restrictions are recorded in `DECISIONS.md` and
`repo/generators/6a289c5b0897/notes.md`.
