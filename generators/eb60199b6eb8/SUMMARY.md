On a zero background, rows are independent. A row with exactly two separated nonzero cells of the same color becomes the inclusive horizontal segment between them on a fresh grid. Empty rows, mismatched pairs, triples, and every other invalid row become all zero.

Generator version `3.0.0` varies 4..10 rows, 5..12 columns, 1..3 valid pair rows, and 3..5 foreground colors. Every ordinary example contains a mismatched pair and a three-cell outer-color match so that the canonical-fitting shortcuts “connect any two” and “connect matching outer cells” are explicitly refuted.
