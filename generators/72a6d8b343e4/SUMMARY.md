# 72a6d8b343e4 — Endpoint Bridge

A zero-background grid contains endpoint groups in distinct nonzero colours. A
colour is bridged only when it appears **exactly twice** and its two cells share
a row or a column; the inclusive straight segment is painted in that colour.
Adjacent pairs are already complete. Misaligned pairs and three-occurrence
groups remain unchanged.

The deterministic curriculum alternates canonical-profile examples
(8×9/10/11, two endpoint colours) with dual-witness examples containing both a
misaligned pair and a guarded three-occurrence group. Thus every three-demo
window and every cyclic query rotation of a four-instance window refutes the
two strongest canonical-fitting rivals: Manhattan-connect every pair, and join
aligned subsets despite an extra occurrence.
