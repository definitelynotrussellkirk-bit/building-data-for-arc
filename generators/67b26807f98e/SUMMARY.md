0-bg grid (size varies). ROW 0 holds a SELECTOR color in 2..7. A FRAME of that color (a rectangular border) encloses a HOLE SHAPE (its interior empty cells; the rest of the interior is filled with the frame color). A color-9 ANCHOR sits where the stamp fits, plus 1-2 DECOY frames of other colors. Rule: read the selector, find the matching frame, take its interior hole cells (normalized), and stamp them in COLOR 8 (cyan) with the shape's top-left at the anchor, on a blank grid. The grid size, selector color, the hole shape, the anchor and the decoys all vary freely, so the output (the stamped hole shape) varies too (was a fixed layout).

Kind: `per_puzzle`. Imported version: `3.1.0`.
